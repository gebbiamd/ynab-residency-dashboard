💜 YNAB Setup Tips for Residency

Categories to create: Make separate categories for each sinking fund above. Set targets on each so YNAB reminds you if you're underfunding them.

Stipends: When your tech ($1,750), food ($1,250), or relocation ($2,500) stipends arrive, treat them as income in YNAB and immediately assign them to relevant categories — don't let them sit unassigned!

Useful YNAB report: Pull a "Spending Totals" report from your current budget (last 3–6 months) filtered by category group. This will let us validate these estimates against your real spending patterns. Export as CSV and share!

Paycheck template: Since residents get paid bi-weekly or semi-monthly, set up a paycheck transaction template. Your Roth IRA contribution should come out right after each paycheck — automate it at your brokerage!
