🔑 Connect Your YNAB Account
This dashboard connects directly to your YNAB budget via their free Personal Access Token API — your data never leaves your device. No login, no third-party storage.
1
Open YNAB → click your profile icon (top right) → Account Settings
2
Scroll to Developer Settings → click "New Token" → give it any name (e.g. "Claude Dashboard")
3
Copy the token and paste it below. Save it somewhere safe — YNAB only shows it once!
🔒 Your token is saved in this browser's localStorage for convenience and is never sent anywhere except directly to api.youneedabudget.com over HTTPS. Use Disconnect to clear it. Revoke it anytime in YNAB Account Settings.
